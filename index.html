Principles

Three promises behind every app.

Clear about your data

No accounts, and what you enter stays on your phone. Where an app uses an outside service, like the analytics and ads in SIP Calculator, its page says so plainly.

Simple, with no tricks

No dark patterns and no hidden settings. Each app does one job and stays out of your way.

Made with care

Every screen is designed and tested by hand, with clear, readable type that works for the whole family.

From the maker

Small apps, looked after properly.

Hi, I’m Ankit. I make Prestia apps on my own, from India.

I started because many everyday apps felt loud, and asked for permissions they didn’t need. I wanted tools my parents could use without help, and that I would trust with my own data.

Each app here is small, focused and maintained. If something breaks, you can write to me and I will read it.
