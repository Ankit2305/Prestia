Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

In short

  • This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising.
  • None of the apps needs an account.
  • Bhakti and Deeplink Tester collect no personal data.
  • SIP Calculator uses Google services for usage analytics, crash reports and ads. The details are below.
  • Your data is never sold.

What this covers

This policy covers the Prestia website and three Android apps: Bhakti, SIP Calculator and Deeplink Tester. Prestia is the name under which Ankit Kumar, an independent developer in India, publishes apps. “We” and “us” below mean him.

Bhakti also has its own, more detailed policy at bhakti.prestia.in/privacy. For Bhakti, that policy is the one that applies.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub. To deliver each page, GitHub receives your IP address and basic details of the request, such as your browser, and may keep them in its logs.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google also receives your IP address when a page loads.

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising. If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser only.

Bhakti

Bhakti has no account, no ads and no analytics. What you save, such as liked tracks, bookmarks, playlists, your daily routine and streak, reminder settings, your chosen city and text size, is stored only on your phone.

The app loads prayer audio and artwork from the servers that host them. Those servers see your IP address and basic device details, as any website would.

It asks for notifications (for reminders and playback controls), optional exact alarms, permission to restore reminders after a restart, background playback and internet access. It does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, camera or files.

SIP Calculator

SIP Calculator has no account. The amounts, rates and periods you enter are calculated on your phone, and the goals and contributions you save are stored only on your phone. We do not receive them.

The app uses these Google services, which receive information under Google’s own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy):

Firebase Analytics records how the app is used: for example when it opens, which screens and calculators you visit, the return rate and number of years in a calculation, a goal’s category, and a rough size band (low, mid or high) when you add a contribution. It does not record your exact amounts or goal names. Google links these events to an app instance ID and basic device details.

Firebase Crashlytics sends a report with device and app details when the app crashes, so problems can be fixed. Firebase Remote Config fetches settings, such as whether ads are shown.

Google AdMob shows banner ads when they are switched on. AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID and usage details to choose and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

The “Suggest a feature” option opens a Google Form in your browser. Anything you type there is sent to us through Google Forms.

Deeplink Tester

Deeplink Tester does not request internet access. The links you open, and the time each was last opened, are stored only on your phone. There is no account, no ads and no analytics.

When you open a link, Android passes it to whichever app handles it. What that app does with the link is up to that app.

Google Play and Android

The apps are distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update them, as described in its own privacy policy.

If Android’s backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the apps’ data in your device backup with Google.

If you email us

If you write to support@prestia.in, your email address and message are used only to reply to you.

Your choices

You can uninstall an app at any time. Data stored only on your phone is removed with it, or when you clear the app’s storage in Android settings.

To ask about or delete any data, write to support@prestia.in.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Bhakti is made for the whole family and collects no personal data from anyone. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, write to support@prestia.in and it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to support@prestia.in.
