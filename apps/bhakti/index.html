Bhakti

Aartis, bhajans and kathas with Devanagari, transliteration and meaning.

Read and listen along in Devanagari, with transliteration and plain-language meaning. A playlist for each day’s deity, gentle reminders and a simple daily routine help you keep a calm practice.

Features

What it does

Devanagari text

Every line in clear Devanagari script, with a text size you can adjust.

Transliteration and meaning

Read along in English letters, with a plain-language meaning for each verse.

Listen or read

Play a recording, or simply read. Aartis, bhajans and kathas are all in one place.

Today’s playlist

A playlist for each day’s deity, like Shiva on Monday, ready when you open the app.

Gentle reminders

A quiet call before sunrise or at the evening aarti, only if you turn them on.

Sunrise and sunset

Today’s times for the city you choose, to plan your morning and evening puja.

Niyama, your daily routine

Tick off morning aarti, japa and sandhya deepam, and keep your streak going.

Playlists and sharing

Like what moves you, build your own playlists and share a reading with family.
