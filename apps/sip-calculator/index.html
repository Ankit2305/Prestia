SIP Calculator
Features

What it does

SIP calculator

Set a monthly amount, return rate and period. See total investment, estimated gains and future value.

Inflation-adjusted SIP

Add an inflation rate to see what your returns could be worth in today’s money.

Lump sum

See how a one-time investment could grow over the same period.

Goal calculator

Start from a target amount and find the monthly SIP you would need.

Goal tracker

Set a goal with an optional deadline, add what you save and watch your progress.

SWP calculator

Plan regular withdrawals from an invested amount.

Loan and EMI

Work out the monthly instalment for a loan amount, rate and tenure.

FD, RD and PPF

Quick estimates for fixed deposits, recurring deposits and PPF.
